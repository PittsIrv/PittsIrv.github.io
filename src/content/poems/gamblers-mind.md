---
title: "The Gambler's Mind"
date: "September 2024"
category: "Poker"
---

Between the shuffle and the deal,
A universe of chance revealed,
Where mathematics meets the soul,
And probability takes its toll.

The cards don't care about my plans,
They fall like time through hourglass sands,
But how I play what I receive—
That's where my character takes leave.

To fold is wisdom sometimes shown,
To raise is courage fully grown,
To call requires calculated thought,
Each action costs, each lesson bought.

The table teaches what books can't say,
That variance will have its way,
But long-term thinking wins the game,
As fleeting luck and skill aren't same.

So shuffle, deal, and let's begin,
Not every hand is meant to win,
The gambler's mind knows this as true:
Success is playing well, not lucky through.
