---
title: "Adventure"
date: "November 10, 2022"
category: "Poetry"
---

Set out on an adventure,
Stop at the dusk, dreams, and cloud.
Meet a colorful river,
And our stories build a bridge.

We part the morning's fine rain,
Board the train of spring,
Ride through the sea of blossoms,
And stop at a carousel's platform.

The evening breeze nudges the clouds,
The sunset pales, fish-belly white.
Two shadows under the moon
Cover the moss along the road.
See?
Eyes are silent confessions.
