---
title: "Digital Dreams"
date: "October 2024"
category: "Technology"
---

In circuits deep and algorithms bright,
Where data flows like rivers through the night,
I find a world of logic, pure and clean,
A binary dance, a digital machine.

Yet in the code, I see a human touch,
The art of solving problems, meaning much,
For every function, every line I write,
Is poetry disguised in black and white.

The bugs we chase, the features we create,
The midnight hours spent to innovate,
Are more than work—they're dreams in code expressed,
A testament to human quest for best.

So let the servers hum their electric song,
In this digital world, we all belong,
For code is not just zeros, ones aligned,
But mirrors of the creative human mind.
