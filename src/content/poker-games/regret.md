---
name: "Regret"
category: "learned"
description: "Strategic grouping game where early decisions limit later options."
quote: "Commit early, regret forever."
rules:
  - "Deal 6 cards to each player"
  - "Before the flop: group cards into 3 groups of 2 cards each (cannot change groups later)"
  - "After flop: discard one group (2 cards)"
  - "After turn: discard another group (2 cards)"
  - "Remaining 2 cards form your final hand for showdown"
  - "Pot limit, bomb pot"
---
