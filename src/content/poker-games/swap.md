---
name: "Swap"
category: "created"
description: "Voting variant where community cards switch between boards."
quote: "Control is an illusion, adaptation is key."
rules:
  - "Double board"
  - "After each betting round (before dealing next card): players vote for on card on each boards"
  - "Majority vote from Board 1 switches places with majority vote from Board 2"
  - "If voting ties 3 times for either board, no swap occurs"
  - "Pot splits between winners of each board"
  - "Pot limit, bomb pot"
---
