description: "Four-card variant with double board and hidden/shown card selection."
quote: "What you show matters as much as what you hide."
rules:
  - "Each player receives 4 cards: 2 face-up (shown), 2 face-down (hidden)"
  - "When making final hand, must use exactly 1 shown card and 1 hidden card"
  - "Double board: pot splits between best hand on each board"
  - "Pot limit, bomb pot"
