---
name: "Majority Vote"
category: "created"
description: "Democratic game where players collectively control which community cards stay."
quote: "Democracy is just organized chaos."
rules:
  - "Double board"
  - "After each betting round (before dealing next card): players vote on each card in both boards"
  - "Any card receiving majority votes is kicked and replaced with a new card from the deck"
  - "If voting ties 3 times in a row, no change occurs"
  - "Pot splits between winners of each board"
  - "Pot limit, bomb pot"
---
