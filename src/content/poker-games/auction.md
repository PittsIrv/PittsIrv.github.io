---
name: "Auction"
category: "created"
description: "Economic variant where players bid to receive additional cards."
quote: "Everything has a price..."
rules:
  - "Standard 2-card starting hands"
  - "On a player's action, they can 'deal' a card from the deck, starting bid at 1BB"
  - "Other players can outbid; highest bidder wins the card and adds to hand"
  - "Limit each player to 1-2 dealt cards and 1-2 bought cards (set before game)"
  - "Money from bids goes directly to the player who dealt the card"
  - "Pot limit, bomb pot"
