description: "Five-card draw variant with dual winning conditions: best poker hand and highest sum."
quote: "Ha! I get a 10!"
rules:
  - "Deal 5 cards to each player"
  - "First betting round"
  - "After first round, players may discard up to 2 cards and draw 2 new cards from the deck"
  - "Normal betting afterwards"
  - "Two winning conditions: Board 1 wins with best poker hand (normal rankings)"
  - "Board 2 wins with highest hand sum where J, Q, K, A count as 0 (max sum = 49)"
  - "Pot splits between winners of each board"
  - "Pot limit, bomb pot"
